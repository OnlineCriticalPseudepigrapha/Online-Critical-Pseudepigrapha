## 5-6 Ezra TEI (2026-10-15)
- Source: Bensly 1895 (PD), PDF pp. 97-102 (5 Ezra, cc. I-II) and 168-178 (6 Ezra, cc. XV-XVI); OCR in raw/editions/bensly_ocr/.
- scripts/bensly_extract.py: verses placed by aligning the OCR to the Clementine Vulgate (la.wikisource, raw/editions/vulgate/) and corrected for common OCR confusions against a Latin word list. 5 Ezra: 88/88 verses (9 boundaries approximate); 6 Ezra: 141/141 (19 approximate). Not proofread.
- Second witness: codex C (Spanish recension), Bensly Appendix I = PDF pp. 179-188, verse breaks from Bensly's margin numbers plus alignment. C lacks 5 Ezra 1:1-3. Variants of M (footnotes) not included.
- scripts/bensly_to_grammateus.py -> raw/editions/bensly_ocr/{5Ezra,6Ezra}.xml (divisions/div format, like PssSol.xml); TEI {5Ezra,6Ezra}_TEI.xml (valid TEI P5, lem = Bensly, rdg = C). The Bensly apparatus is not included.
- To publish: proofread against the PDF, then copy into static/docs and static/docs/tei.

### 5-6 Ezra: hand proofreading done (2026-10-15)
- All Bensly pages read against the scans (main text 97-102, 168-178; Appendix I codex C 179-188) into `raw/editions/bensly_ocr/p{N}.corr.txt`. The automatic OCR correction is switched off when every page has a corr file.
- Codex C kept diplomatic: abbreviation marks as combining macrons (dn̄s, pp̄ls, qm̄). M variants still not included.
- Verse starts fixed by hand: `MAIN_STARTS`/`C_STARTS` in `scripts/bensly_extract.py`, plus `carry_openers()` (a trailing "Et/Qui/Certe" moves to the next verse).
- Result: 5 Ezra 88/88, 6 Ezra 141/141; 0 empty C verses apart from 5 Ezra 1:1-3, which C lacks. Both TEI files validate.
- Remaining "approx" flags on C mostly mean "boundary from margin number", not doubtful text.
