2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Prayer of Manasseh (CAVT 178 / CANT 203) is a short penitential prayer attributed to King Manasseh of Judah during his Babylonian captivity (2 Chron 33:11-13). Extant in Greek in the Septuagint Odes (Ode 12) and the Didascalia Apostolorum, and in Latin as an appendix to the Vulgate. This edition provides the Greek text from H. B. Swete, The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint, Vol. 3 (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1896), pp. 802-804, in parallel with the Clementine Vulgate Latin text.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Greek witnesses include Codex Alexandrinus (A), Codex Turicensis (T), Codex Veronensis (R), and the Didascalia Apostolorum. Latin witnesses include the Clementine Vulgate Appendix and medieval Parisian Bibles.
