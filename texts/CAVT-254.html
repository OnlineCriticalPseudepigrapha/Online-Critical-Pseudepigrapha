2. Overview & Textual Contents

Apocalypsis pseudo-Methodii is indexed in the canonical master concordance as CAVT-254 (CAVT 254). This text belongs to the DANIEL. APOCALYPSES tradition of ancient Jewish and early Christian literature, predominantly preserved in Multi/Other.
