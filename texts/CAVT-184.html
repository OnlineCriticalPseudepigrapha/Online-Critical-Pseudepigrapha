2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Greek Apocalypse of Ezra (CAVT 184 / CANT 204) is a Christian-Jewish apocalypse in which Ezra journeys through heaven and Tartarus, pleading for sinners before the throne of God. Edited from Parisinus gr. 929 by C. von Tischendorf, Apocalypses Apocryphae (Leipzig, 1866), pp. 24-33.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

The date is uncertain; estimates range from the 2nd to the 9th century CE. The work depends on 4 Ezra and belongs to a family of Ezra apocalypses that includes the Vision of Ezra and the Apocalypse of Sedrach.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Paris, Bibliothèque nationale de France, MS gr. 929 (15th cent.), fols. 512-521.
