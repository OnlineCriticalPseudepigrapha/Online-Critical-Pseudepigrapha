2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Byzantine Apocalypse of Daniel (CAVT 197 / CANT 208) is a 9th-century Greek historicized apocalypse recounting Byzantine-Arab conflicts, the rise of the Antichrist, and the final judgment. Edited by F. Macler, L'Apocalypse de Daniel (1895); Klaus Berger (1976); and K. T. Zervos in OTP 1 (1983).

3. Date, Language & Provenance

The apocalypse belongs to the Byzantine tradition of Daniel apocalypses that read political history as prophecy. Its account of the end includes a Jewish messiah figure who turns out to be the Antichrist.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks
