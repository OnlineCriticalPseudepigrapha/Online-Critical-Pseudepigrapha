2. Overview & Textual Contents

5 Ezra consists of chapters 1-2 of the Latin 4 Ezra, forming a Christian prologue condemning the disobedience of Israel and announcing the call of the nations. Critical edition by R. L. Bensly, The Fourth Book of Ezra (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1895).

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Most scholars regard 5 Ezra as a Christian composition of the 2nd or 3rd century CE, later attached to the Latin translation of 4 Ezra. It survives in two Latin recensions (French and Spanish).

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Codex Sangermanensis (S), Codex Ambianensis (A), Codex Complutensis (C), and related Spanish/French Vulgate manuscripts.
