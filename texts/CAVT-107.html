2. Overview & Textual Contents

Introduction

The Prayer of Joseph (Oratio Josephi; CAVT 107; CANT 351) is a Jewish pseudepigraphon preserved only in patristic citations, most notably by Origen (Commentarium in Evangelium Johannis II.31 [188-190] and Philocalia XXIII.15, 19). It portrays the patriarch Jacob as the archangel Israel, an archangelic leader and first-begotten of all creation.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Provenance

Composed in Greek, likely in Roman Egypt during the late first or early second century CE, prior to Origen's citations in the third century.
