2. Overview & Textual Contents

De Adami compositione et nomine (CAVT 10) is a Latin apocryphon recounting the creation of Adam from the eight cosmic substances (earth, sea, sun, clouds, wind, stones, light, Holy Spirit) and the mystical derivation of his name from the four cardinal directions (Anatole, Dysis, Arktos, Mesembria). Edited by Max Förster in Archiv für Religionswissenschaft 11 (1908), pp. 477-478, from London, British Library, MS Royal 5.E.vii (10th cent.) and Harley 3063 (12th cent.).

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

London, British Library, MS Royal 5.E.vii (10th cent.), fol. 70r; MS Harley 3063 (12th cent.), fol. 12v.
