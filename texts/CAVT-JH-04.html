2. Overview & Textual Contents

Aristeas the Exegete is known from one fragment, preserved through Alexander Polyhistor in Eusebius, that summarizes the story of Job and identifies him with Jobab, as in the Septuagint addition at Job 42:17.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

He wrote before the mid-1st c. BCE, when Alexander Polyhistor quoted him; no more precise date or place can be established.
