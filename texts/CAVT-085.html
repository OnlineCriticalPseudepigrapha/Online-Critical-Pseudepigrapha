2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Treatise of Shem (Tractatus Sem, CAVT 85) is an ancient astrological and calendrical calendar text in 12 chapters corresponding to the signs of the Zodiac, predicting agricultural yields, politics, weather, and health based on the zodiacal sign in which the year begins. Preserved in Syriac in Manchester, John Rylands Library, MS Cod. Syr. 44 (15th cent.). Edited and translated by Alphonse Mingana, Some Early Judæo-Christian Documents in the John Rylands Library (Manchester: The University Press, 1917), pp. 24-29, 54-61.
