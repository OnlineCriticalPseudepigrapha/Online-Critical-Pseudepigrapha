2. Overview & Textual Contents

Qumran. Apocryphi de quibus nihil certi proponi potest is indexed in the canonical master concordance as CAVT-270 (CAVT 270). This text belongs to the VARIA tradition of ancient Jewish and early Christian literature, predominantly preserved in Multi/Other.
