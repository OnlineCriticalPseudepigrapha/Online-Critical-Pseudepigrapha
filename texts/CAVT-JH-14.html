2. Overview & Textual Contents

Aristobulus was a Jewish philosopher who addressed a work on the Torah to a Ptolemaic king. The fragments preserved by Clement of Alexandria and Eusebius explain the Bible's anthropomorphisms allegorically, claim that Greek poets and philosophers borrowed from Moses, and discuss the Sabbath and the date of Passover.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Aristobulus is usually dated to the mid-2nd c. BCE, at Alexandria, in the reign of Ptolemy VI Philometor.
