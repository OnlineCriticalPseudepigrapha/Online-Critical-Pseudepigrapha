2. Overview & Textual Contents

Qumran. Proto-Esther (4Q550) (aramaice) is indexed in the canonical master concordance as CAVT-191 (CAVT 191). This text belongs to the ESDRAS - ESTHER - TOBIT - I-V MACCABAEORUM tradition of ancient Jewish and early Christian literature, predominantly preserved in Aramaic.
