2. Overview & Textual Contents

6 Ezra consists of chapters 15-16 of the Latin 4 Ezra, an apocalyptic prophecy of wars, famines, and persecutions preceding the final judgment. Critical edition by R. L. Bensly, The Fourth Book of Ezra (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1895).

3. Date, Language & Provenance

6 Ezra is usually dated to the late 3rd century CE and thought to be Christian. A Greek fragment of 15:57–59 survives in P.Oxy. 1010, so the work was probably written in Greek.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Codex Sangermanensis (S), Codex Ambianensis (A), Codex Complutensis (C), and related Latin witnesses.
