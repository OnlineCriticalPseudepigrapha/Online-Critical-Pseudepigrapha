2. Overview & Textual Contents

The fragments of Thallus the Chronographer (FGrH 256) derive from a universal history in three books written by a Samaritan or Jewish chronographer of the 1st century CE. Cited by Julius Africanus (Chronographiae fr. 50 ap. Syncell. 610) regarding the darkness at the Crucifixion, and by Theophilus of Antioch (Ad Autolycum III.29) on the antiquity of Belus and the Ogygian flood.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Julius Africanus, Chronographiae fr. 50; Theophilus of Antioch, Ad Autolycum III.29; Flavius Josephus, Antiquitates XVIII.167.
