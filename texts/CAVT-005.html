2. Overview & Textual Contents

The lost Greek Apocalypse of Adam is an ancient Jewish or early Jewish-Christian apocryphon known through early Christian testimonia, lists of canonical and apocryphal books, and Armenian translations. It is distinct from both the Greek Apocalypse of Moses and the Coptic Gnostic Apocalypse of Adam (NHC V,5).

According to the Epistle of Barnabas and early patristic references analyzed by Pierre Prigent and M. R. James, the work narrated visions granted to Adam regarding the destruction of the world by flood and fire, and the coming of the Redeemer.
