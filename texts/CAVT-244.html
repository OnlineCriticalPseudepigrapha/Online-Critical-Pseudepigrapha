2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Apocryphon of Ezekiel is known only from quotations and fragments. Its best-known passage, preserved by Epiphanius, is the parable of the lame man and the blind man, which illustrates the joint judgment of body and soul.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

The work is usually considered Jewish and dated before the end of the 1st c. CE. Early Christian writers quote it under Ezekiel's name.
