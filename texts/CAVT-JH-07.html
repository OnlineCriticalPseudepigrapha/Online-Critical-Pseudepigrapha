2. Overview & Textual Contents

The fragment of Theophilus (FGrH 733) is an excerpt from a Hellenistic Jewish historian preserved by Alexander Polyhistor and cited by Eusebius, Praeparatio Evangelica IX.34.19, recounting Solomon's gift of a golden image to the king of Tyre in exchange for the gilded pillar of the Temple.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Theophilus is otherwise unknown; he is usually dated before the mid-1st century BCE, since Alexander Polyhistor quotes him. Whether he was Jewish or a pagan writer drawing on Jewish tradition is disputed.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Eusebius, Praeparatio Evangelica IX.34.19 (citing Alexander Polyhistor).
