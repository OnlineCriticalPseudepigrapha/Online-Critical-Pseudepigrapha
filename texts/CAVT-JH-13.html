2. Overview & Textual Contents

Ezekiel the Tragedian wrote the Exagoge, a Greek tragedy on the exodus in iambic trimeter. Its fragments, preserved by Eusebius from Alexander Polyhistor, include Moses' dream of being enthroned and the description of a phoenix at Elim.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Ezekiel is usually dated to the 2nd c. BCE and probably wrote in Alexandria.
