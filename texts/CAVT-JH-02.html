2. Overview & Textual Contents

Eupolemus wrote a history of the Jewish kings in Greek. The fragments preserved through Alexander Polyhistor in Eusebius and Clement deal mainly with Moses as the first sage, David, and Solomon's letters to the kings of Egypt and Tyre about the building of the Temple.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

He is often identified with the Eupolemus whom Judas Maccabeus sent to Rome (1 Macc 8:17). If so, he was writing in Judea in the mid-2nd c. BCE.
