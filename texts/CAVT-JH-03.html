2. Overview & Textual Contents

Artapanus wrote a romance-like history of the Jews in Egypt. Fragments preserved through Alexander Polyhistor in Eusebius present Abraham, Joseph and especially Moses as founders of Egyptian culture and religion.

3. Date, Language & Provenance

He is generally dated to the 3rd–2nd c. BCE, in Egypt.

Sources for the Texts of Eusebius and Clement
