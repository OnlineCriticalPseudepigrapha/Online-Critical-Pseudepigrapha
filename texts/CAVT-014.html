2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Conflictus Adae et Euae cum Satana (The Book of Adam and Eve / The Combat of Adam and Eve with Satan) is an extensive late antique Christian haggadic romance preserved in Arabic and Ge'ez (Ethiopic Gadla 'Adam wa-Hewan), translated from a lost Syriac or Christian Arabic archetype.

In four expansive books, it describes the life of Adam and Eve after their expulsion from Paradise into the "Cave of Treasures," their repeated attempts to commit suicide out of grief, their constant deception and spiritual trials inflicted by Satan, the transmission of the sacred golden body of Adam onto Noah's Ark, and its eventual burial at Golgotha, "the center of the earth."

3. Date, Language & Provenance

Original Language: Syriac or Christian Arabic.
Date: 5th–6th century CE archetype.
Recensions: Preserved completely in Ethiopic (Ge'ez) and Christian Arabic manuscripts.
