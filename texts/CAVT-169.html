2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Vision of Elijah (CAVT 169) is an apocryphal Latin vision of the punishment of sinners and the topography of Gehinnom / hell, preserved in a single 11th-century manuscript at the Bibliothèque Mazarine (MS 512, fols. 116v-117r). Critical edition published by Dom Donatien De Bruyne in Revue Bénédictine 37 (1925), p. 58.

4. Manuscript Witnesses & Shelfmarks

Paris, Bibliothèque Mazarine, MS 512 (11th century), fols. 116v-117r.
