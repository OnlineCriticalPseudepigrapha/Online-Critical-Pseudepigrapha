2. Overview & Textual Contents

Aphikia (arabice) is indexed in the canonical master concordance as CAVT-165 (CAVT 165). This text belongs to the I-II SAMUEL - I-II REGES - I-II PARALIPOMENA tradition of ancient Jewish and early Christian literature, predominantly preserved in Arabic.

5. Standard Critical Editions & Studies

Codd.: G. GRAF, I, p. 2112212; — U. ZANETTI, Les manuscrits de
Dair Abi Maqôr. Inventaire (Cahiers d'Orientalisme 11), Genève,
1986, p. 18, 62-63, 64: Monastère de Saint-Macaire, n? 15 (= Bibl.
15), saec. XIX ex.; n? 411 (= Hag. 45), an. 1739; n° 414 (= Hag. 48),
an. 1525.
