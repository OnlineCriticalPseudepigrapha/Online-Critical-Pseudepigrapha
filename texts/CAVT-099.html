2. Overview & Textual Contents

The Testament of Abraham is preserved primarily in Greek in two distinct recensions:

  • Recension A (Longer): A fuller, more elaborate narrative focusing on Abraham’s heavenly journey, the judgment of souls, and his refusal to yield his soul to Death.
  • Recension B (Shorter): A concise, more colloquial redaction with distinct apocalyptic and folkloric features.

Primary Greek Manuscripts

  • Recension A:
  • A: Paris, Bibliothèque nationale de France, MS gr. 770 (dated 1304 CE).
  • B: Jerusalem, Greek Patriarchal Library, Saba 259 (11th c.).
  • C: Paris, BnF, MS gr. 1556 (15th c.).
  • D: Oxford, Bodleian Library, MS Canonici Greek 19 (15th c.).
  • Additional witnesses: Paris gr. 1313 (15th c.), Paris gr. 1613 (15th c.), Paris Suppl. gr. 162; Vienna theol. gr. 237.
  • Recension B:
  • E: Paris, BnF, MS gr. 1613 (15th c.).
  • F: Paris, BnF, MS gr. 1313 (15th c.).
  • M: Milan, Biblioteca Ambrosiana, MS gr. 405 (C 210 inf.; 13th c.) — collated by F. Schmidt.
  • Rome, Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, MS Vat. gr. 1801 (15th c.).

Oriental and Slavonic Versions

  • Coptic:
  • Bohairic version: Rome, Vat. copt. 61, fols. 164v–189v (dated 961/2 CE), ed. I. Guidi (1900).
  • Sahidic fragments: Papyrus Köln Inv. Nr. 3221, noted by C. Römer, H. J. Thissen, and M. Weber.
  • Ethiopic (Ge’ez):
  • Falasha (Beta Israel) and Christian recensions: A. Z. Aešcoly (1951); M. Gaguine (1965).
  • Arabic:
  • Extant in multiple Karshuni and Christian Arabic collections (e.g. Dayr Anba Maqar; ed. W. E. Barnes 1892; catalogue by G. Graf 1944).
  • Church Slavonic and Romanian:
  • Romanian translation: Closely reflects Recension A (ed. M. Gaster 1893/1928).
  • South Slavic and East Slavic miscellanies (Sbornik of Tikveš, ed. V. Antik 1978; D. S. Cooper & H. B. Weber 1976; I. Franko 1896–1910; A. Ja. Jatsimirskij 1921).
  • Ukrainian redaction (Slovo o sviatom Avraame).

5. Standard Critical Editions & Studies

  • Schmidt, Francis. Le Testament grec d’Abraham. Introduction, édition critique des deux recensions grecques, traduction. Texte und Studien zum Antiken Judentum (TSAJ) 11. Tübingen: J.C.B. Mohr (Paul Siebeck), 1986.

The definitive critical edition of both Greek recensions (A and B) with facing French translation and comprehensive stemmatic and historical analysis.

  • Delcor, Mathias. Le Testament d’Abraham. Introduction, traduction du texte grec et commentaire de la recension grecque longue, suivi de la traduction des Testaments d’Abraham, d’Isaac et de Jacob d’après les versions orientales. Studia in Veteris Testamenti Pseudepigrapha (SVTP) 2. Leiden: Brill, 1973.

Comprehensive edition and commentary on Greek Recension A. (Review: N. Walter, OLZ 73 [1978]: 260–262).

  • James, Montague Rhodes. The Testament of Abraham: The Greek Text Now First Edited, with an Introduction and Notes... with an Appendix Containing Extracts from the Arabic Version by W. E. Barnes. Texts and Studies (T&S) 2.2. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1892. Repr. Nendeln: Kraus, 1967.

The editio princeps establishing the foundational two-recension division.

  • Stone, Michael E. The Testament of Abraham: The Greek Recensions Translated. Texts and Translations 2, Pseudepigrapha Series 2. Missoula, MT: Society of Biblical Literature, 1972.

Reprints M. R. James’s Greek text of Recensions A and B with facing English translation and notes.

  • Vassiliev, A. “Mors Abrahami.” In Anecdota graeco-byzantina. Moscow: Imperial University, 1893, 1:lvi–lix, 292–308 [Greek Recension A].
  • Denis, Albert-Marie. Concordance grecque des pseudépigraphes d’Ancien Testament. Concordance, corpus des textes, indices. Louvain-la-Neuve: Université Catholique de Louvain, 1987, 825–832 [Full Greek lemmatized concordance].
  • Bauer, Johannes B. “Testamentum Abrahae A, B.” In Index verborum in libris pseudepigraphis usurpatorum, in C. A. Wahl, Clavis librorum Veteris Testamenti apocryphorum philologica. Graz: Akademische Druck- u. Verlagsanstalt, 1972, 647–691.
