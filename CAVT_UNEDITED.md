# CAVT texts not in OCP whose text or version is unedited (per Haelewyck 1998)

Source: raw/cavt/cavt.md (OCR, corrected 2026-10-20). An entry is listed if CAVT marks the text, or one version of it, as "Ineditum/-a/-us", "nondum editus" or "adhuc ineditum".
The status is as of 1998. Several texts may have been edited since; most Qumran items, for example, were later published in DJD.
